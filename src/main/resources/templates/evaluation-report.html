What the metrics mean

All scores range from 0 (worst) to 1 (best). They are produced by a judge model that reads the question, the retrieved passages and the answer, so treat them as a trend indicator, not as proof. Only sampled answers are evaluated.

Faithfulness
Is the answer grounded in the delivered sources? The answer is split into single statements and each is checked against the retrieved passages. Score = supported statements ÷ all statements. A low value means the model added facts that are not in your documents (hallucination).
Answer relevance
Does the answer address what was asked? The judge writes the questions the answer would be a reply to; the score is how similar they are to the real question. Low values mean the answer drifts off-topic or is evasive.
Completeness
Does the answer cover all parts of the question? The question is split into sub-questions (core, background, follow-up) and each is rated as not, partly or fully addressed. Core parts weigh most. Low values mean parts of the question were left unanswered.
Precision@K
Of the top K retrieved passages, the share that is relevant to the question. Low: the retrieval delivers noise.
Recall@K
Of all relevant passages found in the candidate pool (or named in the golden set), the share that made it into the top K. Low: relevant material is missed.
MRR (mean reciprocal rank)
1 ÷ position of the first relevant passage, averaged. 1.0 means the best passage is always first.
MAP (mean average precision)
Rewards ranking relevant passages above irrelevant ones across the whole top K.
NDCG
Like MAP but uses graded relevance (0–3): highly relevant passages should appear before marginal ones.
Reference recall
Golden set only: the share of statements in your reference answer that the produced answer contains.
Topic and tags
The judge rewrites each question into a neutral topic and assigns up to 5 category tags, so you can see what users ask about without storing the raw questions. When building the report, topics that mean the same are merged (by embeddings, or by the judge model if there are no embeddings). Tags are only merged when they are spelled the same.
